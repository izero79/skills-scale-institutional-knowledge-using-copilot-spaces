# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Advisor / Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Technical Advisors provide specialized expertise and validate the technical feasibility of proposed solutions. They guide architecture decisions and help teams navigate complex technical challenges, bridging the gap between business objectives and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Validate technical feasibility of proposed solutions
- Provide architectural guidance and best practices recommendations
- Review technical designs and implementation approaches
- Escalate technical risks and constraints
- Mentor and advise development teams on technical decisions
- Ensure alignment with technical standards and best practices

### Goals
- Enable sound technical decision-making
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Share knowledge and elevate team technical capability
- Prevent architectural pitfalls and design flaws

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Project Manager** for feasibility assessments and risk identification
- **Advises Development Team** on technical approaches, architecture, and implementation strategies
- **Supports Quality Assurance Lead** by defining technical acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks
- **Partners with Product Managers** to validate that proposed solutions are technically viable

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk assessment reports and technical recommendations
- Mentoring sessions and design guidance documentation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons serve as the bridge between the project team and external stakeholders. They gather requirements, communicate project status, manage expectations, and ensure stakeholder buy-in throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify stakeholder requirements and concerns
- Communicate project status, milestones, and progress to stakeholders
- Manage stakeholder expectations and address concerns proactively
- Ensure stakeholder sign-off and approval on deliverables
- Provide feedback from stakeholders back to the project team
- Coordinate stakeholder engagement activities

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and satisfaction
- Capture complete and accurate requirements from stakeholders
- Maintain transparent communication and trust
- Facilitate timely decision-making and approvals

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager** with stakeholder feedback, concerns, and approval status
- **Provides Business Analyst input** to Product Managers from stakeholder perspective
- **Coordinates with Communication Coordinator** for consistent messaging and updates
- **Works with Developers** to communicate technical constraints to stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder status updates and newsletters
- Requirements gathering sessions and interviews
- Approval documentation and sign-off records

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads own quality standards and acceptance criteria definition. They establish testing strategies, oversee quality execution, and validate that deliverables meet established quality benchmarks and customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria with the team
- Develop and oversee testing strategy and execution plans
- Validate deliverables meet quality benchmarks before release
- Report quality metrics, defects, and improvement opportunities
- Collaborate on test case design and coverage
- Identify and escalate quality risks

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable deliverables
- Maximize customer satisfaction through quality
- Continuously improve quality processes and standards
- Prevent defects from reaching production

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Project Manager** on quality planning and release readiness
- **Works with Development Team** on test case definition and acceptance criteria
- **Coordinates with Technical Advisor** to define technical quality standards
- **Reports to Product Managers** on quality status and impact on customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- Test plans and acceptance criteria documentation
- Quality gate reviews and release checklists

---

## Risk Manager

### Role Summary
Risk Managers proactively identify, monitor, and manage project risks. They help the team anticipate potential issues, develop mitigation strategies, and ensure risks are communicated and addressed throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Proactively identify potential project risks and dependencies
- Assess risk probability, impact, and mitigation strategies
- Maintain and update risk registers
- Escalate critical risks and blockers to appropriate decision-makers
- Monitor risk status and effectiveness of mitigation strategies
- Facilitate risk discussions and contingency planning

### Goals
- Minimize project disruptions from unforeseen issues
- Enable proactive problem-solving
- Ensure informed decision-making about risk trade-offs
- Protect project schedule, scope, and quality

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Project Manager** on risk identification and escalation
- **Advises Technical Advisor** on technical risk assessment
- **Communicates with Stakeholder Liaison** about risks affecting deliverables
- **Consults with Development Team** on technical and execution risks

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and mitigation plans
- Risk assessment reports and escalation notifications
- Risk review meeting facilitation

---

## Communication Coordinator

### Role Summary
Communication Coordinators manage internal and external project communications. They ensure consistent, timely, and clear information flow to all stakeholders, maintaining alignment and transparency throughout the project.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain project communication plans
- Manage internal team communications and announcements
- Coordinate external stakeholder communications and updates
- Maintain communication channels and documentation repositories
- Ensure message consistency across all communications
- Track communication effectiveness and stakeholder feedback

### Goals
- Maintain transparency and alignment across all stakeholders
- Ensure timely and accurate information distribution
- Reduce miscommunication and misalignment
- Build trust and engagement through clear communication

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Coordinates with Project Manager** on communication strategy and timing
- **Partners with Stakeholder Liaison** for external messaging
- **Works with all team members** to collect and distribute project updates
- **Supports Risk Manager** in escalating critical communications
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead** on quality status communications

### Typical Communication
- Status update newsletters and announcements
- Communication plans and distribution schedules
- Centralized project documentation and dashboards

---

## Dependencies Manager

### Role Summary
Dependencies Managers track and manage cross-project dependencies and integrations. They identify where the project relies on other teams or systems, coordinate with external teams, and ensure smooth handoffs and integrations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document internal and external project dependencies
- Track dependency status and potential blocking issues
- Coordinate with external teams and manage integration points
- Plan and schedule handoffs and integration activities
- Escalate dependency risks and resolve blockers
- Maintain dependency mapping and integration documentation

### Goals
- Ensure smooth coordination across projects and teams
- Prevent delays caused by unmanaged dependencies
- Enable parallel work and efficient resource utilization
- Maintain clear visibility into integration points

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Coordinates with Project Manager** on dependency planning and scheduling
- **Works with Risk Manager** to identify and manage dependency risks
- **Communicates with Technical Advisor** on technical integration challenges
- **Engages with Stakeholder Liaison** on dependencies affecting deliverables
- **Collaborates with Development Team** on technical integration planning

### Typical Communication
- Dependency maps and integration plans
- Coordination meeting notes and escalation reports
- Integration schedule and handoff documentation
